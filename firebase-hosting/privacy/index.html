Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Invite Someone App ("Invite") helps people discover compatible people and activities and send thoughtful local invitations. This policy explains how Invite handles information used to provide the service.

Information we use

  • Account information such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, such as your name, city, interests, availability, connection goals, biography, and profile image reference.
  • Invite activity data such as activities you create or join, invitations, saved activities, and related account preferences.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the service.

Google sign-in data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Invite uses Google authentication to receive the basic identity information needed to sign you in, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information made available by the sign-in flow. Invite does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or other Google product data.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, create and maintain your Invite profile, provide discovery and invitation features, enforce authorization and safety rules, maintain service reliability, and respond to support or security issues.

Storage and sharing

Invite uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the service, including Firebase Authentication for managed identity, an Invite API for application authorization and business logic, and MongoDB Atlas for Invite application data. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate these functions or when required by law.

Data choices

You can choose what profile information to provide and can stop using Invite at any time. Account export and deletion controls are part of Invite's production-readiness work; until those controls are available in-product, contact the developer using the support email shown in the Google/Firebase application listing for account or privacy requests.

Security and retention

Invite uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and application data. Information is retained only as needed to provide and secure the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Invite evolves. The current version will be published at this URL with its updated date.
